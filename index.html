What I do now

I sell growth, and then I go and build it

Senior App Growth Manager, Emerging Markets
Google · Dublin

I own the growth strategy for a portfolio of 20+ top businesses across Israel, the Middle East and Africa, working with the people who carry the number: CEOs, CMOs and CFOs. My job is to turn what Google’s app and AI technology can do into profit they can point at on a P&L.

That means the strategy, not the demo. Where should the next marketing euro go? Which market is worth entering this year, and what does entry actually cost? What does the model have to return before finance will fund it? I answer those questions, agree the plan in the room, and stay on the hook for the result.

Before Google I did the same work from the other side of the table: launching fintech products, negotiating with KYC and verification vendors, and delivering a $1M+ education programme in Rwanda. Selling a plan and shipping one are the same discipline at different hours of the day.
